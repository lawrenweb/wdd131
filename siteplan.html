Website Planning Document

Site Name

Talamaivao Fitness Hub

This website provides fitness information, workout plans, nutrition advice, and healthy lifestyle tips for people looking to improve their health and fitness.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help users learn about fitness, discover beginner workout routines, understand healthy nutrition habits, and stay motivated on their fitness journey.

Scenarios

  • What workout routine should I follow as a beginner?
  • What foods should I eat to support my fitness goals?
  • How often should I exercise each week?
  • Where can I contact the website owner for more information?

Color Schema

#1D3557

Navy Blue will be used for headings and navigation elements.

#F1FAEE

Light Gray will be used for backgrounds and content sections.

#F4A261

Orange will be used for highlights and buttons.

Typography

Poppins - Headings and titles

Arial - Body text and paragraphs
